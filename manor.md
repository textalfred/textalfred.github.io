Alfred runs one household at a time, and each one gets its own private manor. Nobody else's butler knows your business.

## one household, one manor

Everyone in your household gets their own Alfred: a separate process with its own credentials, its own memory and its own saved places. It's a wall, not a filter. A guest's Alfred cannot see the owner's finances, calendar or Swiggy account, and an Alfred that can't prove whose it is refuses to start.

## your data stays yours

What he keeps: the transactions from the statements you send him, the places you've saved, the reminders you've set. Nothing is sold, and nothing is shared with another household. Want it gone? Tell us and we delete it.

To be plain about who else touches it:

- your messages are handled by AI providers (Anthropic and OpenAI);
- Hindi speech is generated by Sarvam;
- the service runs on a cloud server.

## why the waitlist

We're letting people in slowly, so every Alfred is actually good at being someone's Alfred. Each new household gets set up properly: accounts linked, addresses right, the cook's number saved.

The waitlist asks for your name, your WhatsApp number and, if you like, the first boring thing you'd hand off. We send one WhatsApp message, when your room is ready. That's all.

Even the Batcave had a guest list.
