Spend a rupee without your word. That's the one thing Alfred will never do.

## the one-time code

When he's built a cart, he doesn't ask "shall I go ahead?" He sends you a short one-time code, four characters, good for that order and nothing else. The order runs only when your message is exactly that code.

That check happens before the AI ever reads your message. There is no setting he can flip and nothing he can decide on your behalf. "Yes please", "sure" and "go on then" place nothing. The code does.

## why not a "confirm?" button

Because a button asks the AI whether you agreed, and that is precisely the question an AI shouldn't be answering. A guess about what you meant is still a guess. The code isn't.

## the second gate

Then you pay with your own UPI PIN, in your own bank app. Alfred never sees it. Two gates, and he's inside neither.

## what he can read, and what he can do

- **Read:** your spending, your calendar, the documents and reels you send him.
- **Do, only once you've said so:** place an order, send an invite, cancel a meeting.
- **Never:** move money, ask you for a verification code, or invent a phone number, an address or a link.

Each person also has a spending cap, checked before he's consulted at all.

A butler who acts on assumptions is just a very expensive intern.
