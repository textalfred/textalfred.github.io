## how it goes

Send him the document nobody wants to read: the lease, the policy, the 1,990-page annual report. Ask a question. He searches it, finds the page, and quotes the clause, so you can check his work.

Photos work the same way. A bill, a menu, a prescription, a sign in a language you don't read. And if typing is too much, send a voice note. He understands English, Hindi, and the Hinglish in between.

## what he needs from you

- The file, the photo or the voice note, sent to him on WhatsApp.
- A question, if you want more than a summary.

## what he won't do

- Pretend he read every word. On a file that size he searches, and he'll tell you that's what he did.
- Paraphrase when a quote will do. You get the page number and the words.
