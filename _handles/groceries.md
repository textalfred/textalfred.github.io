## how it goes

You message him the way you'd message a person. "Two diet cokes and a pack of atta Maggi." "The usual." "Dinner from the Andhra place, not too spicy." He searches Instamart or Swiggy, builds the cart, and tells you the total in rupees before anything happens.

Then he sends a one-time code. Reply with exactly that code and the order goes through. You pay with your own UPI PIN in your own bank app, and the delivery lands at the door. No app to open, no cart to fiddle with.

## what he needs from you

- A linked Swiggy account.
- A delivery address.
- The code, typed back, when you're happy with the cart.

## what he won't do

- Place an order because you sounded keen. No code, no order.
- Touch your UPI PIN. That stays between you and your bank.
- Swap in something you didn't ask for without saying so first.
