## how it goes

You type what you'd say, in English, Hinglish, or half a sentence. He turns it into a proper spoken Hindi voice note and sends it on WhatsApp, the way anyone else in the house would. Not a text she'd have to squint at. A voice she can play while the pressure cooker goes.

He can do it once ("we're eating out tonight") or on a schedule ("every weekday at 6pm, ask what's for dinner"). Either way, you don't have to remember, and she doesn't have to decode.

## what he needs from you

- The cook's WhatsApp number, saved once.
- What you want said. He handles the Hindi.

## what he won't do

- Message anyone you haven't asked him to. The schedules you set are the only exception.
- Make phone calls. It's voice notes or nothing.
- Improvise the menu. If you didn't say paneer, there is no paneer.
