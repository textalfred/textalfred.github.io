## how it goes

He reads your Google Calendar, so "what's on tomorrow?" gets a straight answer. Ask him to set up a call, move one, or clear an afternoon, and he does it, with a Google Meet link attached. All-day events too.

For a meeting that repeats, he asks whether you mean this one or the whole series. Cancelling the entire standup when you meant Thursday's is not a mistake he intends to make.

## what he needs from you

- Your Google Calendar, connected once.
- Email addresses for the people you're inviting. A name isn't an address.

## what he won't do

- Send an invite or cancel a meeting without your say-so. Same rule as the groceries: he asks, you answer.
- Guess someone's email from their name.
- Accept things on your behalf. Your diary, your decisions.
