## how it goes

Forward him the reel, or the carousel, and ask what you actually wanted to know. The recipe. The name of the place. The three books she held up. He reads the caption, listens to what's said (Hindi included), and reads the text flashed on screen, then answers in plain words.

Carousels too, every slide of them. You get the one useful thing, without the forty-second build-up.

## what he needs from you

- The link, forwarded to him on WhatsApp.
- A question. "What's this?" works; "what's the recipe?" works better.

## what he won't do

- Make things up to fill a gap. If the quantities weren't in the reel, he'll say so.
- Post, like or comment on anything. He watches; he doesn't participate.
