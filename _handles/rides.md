## how it goes

You name the place, the way you'd tell a driver. He looks it up, finds the right address, and sends a link. Tap it, and Uber opens with the destination filled in. You pick the ride and go.

No typing addresses into a tiny box. No choosing between four places with the same name. He does the knowing; you do the tapping. Not quite the Batmobile, but it does come to the door.

## what he needs from you

- Uber on your phone, already signed in.
- Where you're going. "Home" and "the office" work once he knows them.

## what he won't do

- Book or pay for the ride. That tap is yours, in Uber.
- Invent an address. If he can't find the place, he says so rather than send you somewhere plausible.
