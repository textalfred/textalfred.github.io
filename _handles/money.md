## how it goes

He's read your statements. All of them. Ask the way you'd ask a friend who kept the receipts: "What did I spend on food in August?", "How much went to Uber this quarter?", "Who's this ₹4,750 to?" He answers with a total, the top merchants, and the transactions behind it.

UPI transfers to "A SHARMA 7XXXX" become people with names. Transfers between your own accounts and your salary are recognised, so the totals are the real ones.

## what he needs from you

- Your statement PDFs, sent to him on WhatsApp.

## what he won't do

- Move money. He can read your spending; he cannot touch it.
- Guess at a statement he doesn't recognise. He'll tell you it's not a format he reads, rather than invent the numbers.
- Share any of it. Your statements live in your manor, not anyone else's.
