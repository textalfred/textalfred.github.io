## how it goes

You say it the way you'd say it to a person: "remind me Sunday", "in two hours", "every Friday evening", "the first of every month". He works out when that is in your timezone and writes it down. When the time comes, the reminder turns up in the same WhatsApp chat, where you'll actually see it.

No reminders app, no alarms to label, no calendar clutter. One line in, one line out, on time.

## what he needs from you

- What to remind you about, and roughly when. If "later" is too vague, he'll ask.

## what he won't do

- Nag. One reminder, at the time you asked for.
- Remind other people on your behalf, unless you ask him to message them.
- Forget. That's rather the point of him.
